

# Presentation Theme Guide

## 1. Visual identity

**Theme name:** Dark Technical Cyan  
*Descriptive name for this guide, not an official brand-template name.*

The reference style combines black backgrounds, white typography, bright cyan accents, angular panels, and technology imagery. It has a technical product-briefing character, with structured layouts for instructions, deployment options, and supporting evidence.

## 2. Canvas and layout grid

| Property | Reference specification |
|---|---|
| Slide format | 16:9 widescreen, 13.333 × 7.5 inches. |
| Main horizontal margins | Approximately 0.64 inches on both sides. |
| Standard title position | X: 0.64 inches, Y: 0.65 inches. |
| Title width | Approximately 12.05 inches. |
| Content region | Generally begins below the title/subtitle and ends above the bottom branding area. |
| Alignment | Predominantly left-aligned text, with aligned columns and consistent panel edges. |
| Footer | Small slide number at bottom left and white brand lockup at bottom right. |

**Application recommendation:** Keep this grid stable across the deck. Adapt the content arrangement to the message rather than repeating one layout on every slide.

## 3. Color system

### Core palette

| Role | Color | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | `#000000` | Main content-slide background. |
| Primary text | `#FFFFFF` | Titles and body copy. |
| Primary accent | `#00C2DE` | Subtitles, section labels, icons, connectors, and short emphasis rules. |
| Neutral gray | `#9D9FA2` | Supporting neutral emphasis, including the rollout banner. |
| Dark teal | `#004A54` | Subdued progress indicators alongside cyan. |
| Occasional highlight | `#FFC000` | Small note or caution marker. |

Dark charcoal fills and thin gray outlines distinguish panels from the black canvas without creating strong visual separation.

**Application recommendations:**
- Keep most of each slide black or dark neutral.
- Use cyan selectively to establish hierarchy.
- Reserve yellow for a small, meaningful note.
- Avoid introducing a competing dominant accent color.
- Use approximately `#262626` for charcoal panels when an exact source shape is unavailable.

## 4. Typography

| Element | Reference treatment |
|---|---|
| Main font family | Arial for both heading and body theme fonts. |
| Cover title | Approximately 40 pt, bold, with white and cyan text. |
| Standard slide title | 28 pt Arial Bold, white. |
| Subtitle | 20–24 pt, regular weight, cyan. |
| Panel heading | Typically 16–20 pt, bold, cyan. |
| Body copy | Typically 14–18 pt, regular, white. |
| Step number | 24 pt, bold, cyan. |
| Customer quotation | 20 pt Times New Roman, bold italic, white. |
| Source note | Approximately 11 pt. |
| Legal text | 14 pt. |

**Application recommendations:**
- Use 18 pt body text where practical for new presentation content.
- Keep smaller sizes for dense technical detail or notes.
- Shorten or split content before reducing font size.
- Use the serif quotation treatment only for a genuine attributed quotation.
- Keep titles concise and avoid unnecessary all-caps text.

## 5. Shapes, lines, and framing

The recurring geometric motif is a rectangular panel with a diagonally clipped corner. Some panels use clipped opposite corners, while others use a single clipped corner.

Thin separators, short cyan rules, and restrained cyan connectors organize the content. Panels use dark fills and subtle outlines rather than heavy borders or prominent shadows.

**Application recommendations:**
- Use angular panels when they clarify a group or sequence.
- Keep line weights light, approximately 0.5–1 pt.
- Maintain a consistent clipped-corner size.
- Avoid rounded cards, pill labels, glossy buttons, and decorative shadows.
- Use open columns when panel borders add no information.

## 6. Imagery and icons

The cover pairs an oversized, subdued brand symbol with cyan technology imagery and a title positioned across the center-right area.

Content imagery includes cool-toned enterprise technology photographs, actual software screenshots, and simple cyan line icons.

**Application recommendations:**
- Favor dark, cool-toned images that work naturally with black and cyan.
- Use genuine screenshots for software demonstrations.
- Keep screenshots large enough for the relevant interface detail to remain readable.
- Use a consistent outline-icon family.
- Preserve image and logo proportions.
- Keep decorative imagery away from text-heavy areas.
- Use approved logo artwork rather than recreating a logo from shapes.

## 7. Reusable layout library

### A. Cover

**Reference composition:** Oversized muted brand symbol on the left, technology imagery on the right, and a compact white/cyan title block overlapping the central area.

**Use for:** Opening a product, platform, or technical briefing.

### B. Step-by-step process

**Reference composition:** Four narrow panels across the slide, each with a cyan icon, cyan heading, white bullet list, and step number near the bottom. Small cyan connectors indicate sequence, with an outcome strip below.

**Use for:** Setup instructions, onboarding, or a short sequential workflow.

### C. Explanation with software demonstration

**Reference composition:** Stacked icon-led explanations on the left, a large software screenshot on the upper right, and a compact action panel below the screenshot.

**Use for:** Installation instructions or feature demonstrations.

### D. Three-part benefits or deployment options

**Reference composition:** White title and cyan subtitle, two short supporting statements near the top, then three aligned image-led panels with cyan headings and short white descriptions.

**Use for:** Benefits, deployment scenarios, or comparable options.

### E. Context plus implementation steps

**Reference composition:** A framed context panel on the left and three open step columns on the right, separated by thin vertical rules and marked with small cyan accents.

**Use for:** Architecture choices, prerequisites, or implementation guidance.

### F. Availability comparison

**Reference composition:** Two large side-by-side panels with cyan headings, aligned capability rows, dark label blocks, and a full-width summary banner beneath.

**Use for:** Current versus planned capabilities or two comparable release phases.

### G. Customer evidence

**Reference composition:** White title and cyan metric subtitle, a wide charcoal quotation band with a customer logo at left, and five compact capability columns below. A small source note sits near the bottom.

**Use for:** Customer feedback, adoption evidence, or survey findings.

### H. Legal and closing slides

**Reference composition:** The legal slide uses a standard white title and plain white paragraphs on black. The closing slide uses a large centered white logo on an otherwise empty black canvas.

**Use for:** Required notices and a restrained brand close.

## 8. Brand and content boundaries

The reference deck includes AMD branding in the footer and a logo-only closing slide.

**Application recommendations:**
- Retain AMD assets only when appropriate for the new presentation.
- For another organization, replace the logo and legal language while preserving the layout system.
- Apply the correct classification label for the new material.
- Reuse visual patterns without carrying forward old metrics, release dates, quotations, or product claims.
- Keep titles, body text, tables, and diagrams editable.

## 9. Exceptions and extensions

The reference includes a light webpage screenshot and a dark browser screenshot presented almost full-slide. These are demonstration assets rather than alternative master-slide themes.

**Recommendations for new content:**
- Preserve original screenshot colors when needed for accuracy.
- Keep the surrounding slide design consistent with the dark theme.
- For charts, extend the palette with cyan as the primary series and neutral grays for comparisons.
- Prefer direct labels and light gridlines.
- For tables, use dark backgrounds, white text, and restrained cyan headers.
- Treat these chart and table rules as extensions, not extracted chart templates.

## 10. Reuse instruction

For a future generation request, paste this guide and add:

> Create a presentation about **[TOPIC]** for **[AUDIENCE]**, containing **[NUMBER]** slides. Follow the theme guide above. Select layouts according to each slide’s purpose, maintain consistent typography and margins, and keep content editable. Use only supplied or verified facts. Treat cited slides as visual references, not as sources of claims for the new presentation.
